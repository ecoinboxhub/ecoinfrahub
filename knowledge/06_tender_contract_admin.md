# Tender and Contract Administration

## Procurement Methods
- Open tender: Public advertisement, any qualified contractor may bid
- Selective tender: Pre-qualified list of contractors invited to bid
- Design-bid-build: Traditional method, sequential design then construction
- Design-build: Single contractor responsible for design and construction
- EPC/turnkey: Contractor provides engineering, procurement, construction
- Framework agreement: Pre-negotiated terms for multiple similar projects

## Tender Documentation
- Invitation to tender: Project description, tender period, submission requirements
- Instructions to tenderers: Bid format, pricing schedule, qualification criteria
- Conditions of contract: FIDIC, NEC, JCT or other standard forms
- Specification: Technical requirements, standards, testing, workmanship
- Bill of quantities: Measured items with estimated quantities for pricing
- Drawings: Contract drawings showing scope, dimensions, and details

## Tender Evaluation
- Responsiveness check: Complete submission, signed, with required securities
- Technical evaluation: Methodology, program, resources, quality plan, past experience
- Financial evaluation: Adjusted bid price, arithmetic errors corrected, discounts
- Most economically advantageous tender (MEAT): Quality and price ratio weighting
- Abnormally low bids: Detailed cost analysis required if below 80% of engineer's estimate
- Post-qualification: Confirm capabilities and resources of lowest evaluated bidder

## Contract Administration
- Engineer's representative: Day-to-day supervision, inspection, measurement
- Site instructions: Written instructions for variations, additional work, changes
- Variations: Cost and time impact assessed, variation order issued before work
- Measurement and valuation: Monthly interim payment certificates for work done
- Quality control: Inspection and testing, hold points, non-conformance reporting
- Progress monitoring: S-curves, milestone tracking, delay analysis

## Payment Mechanisms
- Advance payment: Mobilization advance up to 15% of contract sum, repaid by deductions
- Interim payments: Monthly valuation of work completed, materials on site
- Retention: 5-10% of each certificate, half released at practical completion
- Final account: All variations and adjustments, final measurement, claims assessed
- Price adjustment: Formula method for contracts over 12 months duration
- Performance bond: 5-10% of contract sum, valid until defects liability period ends

## Claims Management
- Extension of time: Delays beyond contractor control, assessed under contract provisions
- Additional cost: Prolongation costs, disruption, loss of productivity
- Force majeure: Exceptional events, entitlement to time extension and sometimes cost
- Acceleration: Directed or constructive acceleration, premium cost recovery
- Claims submission: Notice within 28 days, full particulars within 42 days per FIDIC
- Claim assessment: Entitlement under contract, causation, quantum of cost and time

## Dispute Resolution
- Adjudication: 28-day binding decision by independent adjudicator
- Mediation: Non-binding facilitation to reach settlement
- Dispute board: Standing board for large projects, decisions binding unless revised
- Arbitration: Final and binding, governed by Arbitration Act or institutional rules
- Litigation: Court proceedings, public hearing, appeals process

## Closeout and Handover
- Practical completion: Works complete, snag list prepared, certificates issued
- Defects liability period: Typically 12 months, contractor rectifies defects
- Operation and maintenance manuals: As-built drawings, equipment manuals, spares lists
- Final measurement: Joint measurement of all work, agreed quantities for final account
- Final certificate: Issued after defects corrected, final account agreed, retention released
