# Wastewater Treatment Systems

## Wastewater Characteristics
- Domestic wastewater: Organic matter (BOD 200-400 mg/L), suspended solids (200-350 mg/L)
- Industrial wastewater: Variable composition, may contain heavy metals, oils, chemicals
- BOD:COD ratio: 0.5-0.7 for domestic sewage (readily biodegradable)
- Nitrogen: 20-60 mg/L total, Phosphorus: 5-15 mg/L in domestic sewage
- Fecal coliform: 10^6-10^8 CFU/100mL in raw sewage

## Treatment Levels
- Preliminary: Screening (bar racks 6-25 mm), grit removal, flow equalization
- Primary: Sedimentation tanks, removal of 50-70% TSS, 25-35% BOD
- Secondary: Biological treatment, 85-95% BOD and TSS removal
- Tertiary: Nutrient removal, disinfection, filtration for reuse

## Primary Treatment Design
- Rectangular sedimentation tanks: Length 20-40 m, depth 2-4 m, detention time 1.5-2.5 h
- Circular clarifiers: Diameter 10-40 m, side water depth 2.5-4 m
- Surface overflow rate: 30-50 m3/m2/day for primary sedimentation
- Weir loading: 125-500 m3/m/day for effluent collection
- Sludge production: 0.15-0.25 m3 per 1000 m3 of wastewater treated

## Biological Treatment Methods
- Activated sludge: Aeration tank 4-8 h HRT, MLSS 2000-4000 mg/L, F/M ratio 0.2-0.5
- Trickling filters: Depth 1.5-2.5 m, stone/plastic media, organic loading 0.3-1.0 kg BOD/m3/day
- Oxidation ponds: 3-5 ponds in series, depth 1.0-1.5 m, HRT 15-30 days
- Anaerobic treatment: UASB reactors, HRT 6-12 h for high-strength wastewater
- MBBR: Moving bed biofilm reactors, 50-70% filling ratio with plastic carriers

## Sludge Treatment
- Thickening: Gravity thickeners (5-10% solids), DAF thickeners (3-6% solids)
- Anaerobic digestion: Mesophilic 35 deg C, 15-25 days HRT, biogas yield 0.6-1.0 m3/kg VS
- Aerobic digestion: 40-60 days at 20 deg C, 60-70% volatile solids reduction
- Dewatering: Belt press (15-25% solids), centrifuge (20-30% solids), drying beds (40-60% solids)
- Sludge disposal: Agricultural application, landfill, incineration (if contaminated)

## Natural Treatment Systems
- Constructed wetlands: Horizontal/vertical flow, 0.1-0.5 m depth, HRT 5-14 days
- Waste stabilization ponds: Anaerobic + facultative + maturation ponds
- Duckweed ponds: Nutrient recovery, biomass production
- Land treatment: Slow rate irrigation, overland flow for small communities

## Design Considerations for Developing Countries
- Low-cost technologies: WSPs, septic tanks, constructed wetlands preferred
- Operation simplicity: Minimal mechanical equipment, gravity flow where possible
- Local materials: Clay liners instead of geotextiles, local aggregates for filter media
- Climatic factors: Higher temperatures increase biological activity rates
- Sludge management: Drying beds with underdrains, agricultural reuse after stabilization

## Effluent Standards
- BOD: < 20-30 mg/L for surface water discharge (WHO/FEPA standards)
- TSS: < 30-50 mg/L for surface water discharge
- Fecal coliform: < 1000 CFU/100mL for unrestricted irrigation
- pH: 6.5-8.5 for all discharges
- Total nitrogen: < 10-15 mg/L for sensitive water bodies
- Total phosphorus: < 2-5 mg/L for eutrophication control
