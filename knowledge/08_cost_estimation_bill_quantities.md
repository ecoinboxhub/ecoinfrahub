# Cost Estimation and Bill of Quantities

## Cost Estimation Types
- Order of magnitude estimate: +-30% accuracy, based on similar project benchmarks
- Preliminary estimate: +-20% accuracy, based on unit rates per square meter or bed
- Detailed estimate: +-10% accuracy, based on measured quantities and current rates
- Budget estimate: For client funding approval, contingency 15-25%
- Tender estimate: For contractor bid preparation, based on resource costing
- Final account: Actual costs measured, valued, and agreed at project completion

## Bill of Quantities Structure
- Preliminaries: Site establishment, insurances, bonds, management costs
- General items: Temporary works, traffic management, environmental protection
- Measured works: Earthworks, pavements, concrete, drainage items
- Dayworks: Labor and plant charged at agreed rates for unspecified work
- Provisional sums: Allowances for undefined works, adjusted when designed
- Prime cost sums: For nominated subcontractors or specialist suppliers

## Measurement Principles
- CESMM4: Civil Engineering Standard Method of Measurement, UK standard
- SMM7: Standard Method of Measurement for building works
- Work classification: Class A (excavation), Class E (concrete), Class K (pipework)
- Measurement rules: Net quantities, no allowance for waste or overlap
- Dimension units: Metres (m), square metres (m2), cubic metres (m3), tonnes (t), number (nr)
- Description: Full specification reference, dimensions, workmanship requirements

## Rate Build-Up Components
- Labour cost: Hourly wage + allowances + insurances + overhead allocation
- Plant cost: Ownership (depreciation, interest) + operating (fuel, maintenance, tyres)
- Material cost: Supply price + delivery + storage + wastage allowance
- Subcontractor cost: Quoted price + supervision + accommodation
- Overhead: Site overhead (staff, cabins, utilities) + head office overhead (5-15%)
- Profit margin: Typically 5-15% depending on risk, competition, and market conditions

## Unit Rate Calculation Example
- Excavation over site: Plant (excavator 30t) = 2,500 NGN per hour, output 80 m3/hour = 31 NGN/m3
- Operator cost: 800 NGN per hour / 80 m3/hour = 10 NGN/m3
- Fuel cost: 20 L/hour at 600 NGN/L = 12,000 NGN/hour / 80 m3 = 150 NGN/m3
- Total direct cost: 31 + 10 + 150 = 191 NGN/m3
- Add 10% overhead = 19 NGN/m3, 8% profit = 15 NGN/m3
- Unit rate: 191 + 19 + 15 = 225 NGN/m3

## Price Adjustment Formulas
- Baxter formula: Adjustment factor = 0.4 + 0.2(L/L0) + 0.2(P/P0) + 0.2(M/M0)
- Indices: Labour (L), Plant (P), Materials (M) with base values subscript 0
- Base date indices: From national statistics bureau at tender date
- Current indices: Published monthly, applied to interim valuations
- Non-adjustable element: Typically 40% (profit, overhead, fixed components)

## Contingency Allowances
- Design contingency: 5-15% for incomplete design at time of estimate
- Physical contingency: 5-10% for unforeseen ground conditions, weather
- Price contingency: 5-10% for inflation beyond normal escalation
- Total contingency: Typically 10-20% for preliminary estimates, 5-10% for detailed estimates
- Management reserve: Client-held contingency, released only by project board approval

## Cost Control During Construction
- Cost code structure: WBS-aligned coding for cost tracking by work element
- Monthly cost reports: Budget vs actual (committed, incurred, forecast)
- Cash flow forecasting: S-curve of projected expenditure, payment applications
- Value engineering: Cost reduction proposals without compromising quality/function
- Change order management: Budget impact assessed before change approval
- Cost to complete: Revised forecast based on actual progress and remaining work
