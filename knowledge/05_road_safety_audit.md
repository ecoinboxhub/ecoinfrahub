# Road Safety Audit

## Audit Stages
- Stage 1: Feasibility and preliminary design (alignment options, junction types)
- Stage 2: Detailed design (signage, barriers, lighting, junction geometry)
- Stage 3: Pre-opening (just before traffic is allowed on the completed road)
- Stage 4: Post-opening monitoring (6-12 months after opening, identify accident patterns)

## Key Safety Elements to Review
- Horizontal alignment: Curve radius, superelevation, transition lengths
- Vertical alignment: Sight distance at crests and sag curves, gradient consistency
- Cross-section: Lane and shoulder widths, crossfall, superelevation transitions
- Junctions: Visibility splays, turning radii, channelization, traffic island placement
- Pedestrian facilities: Crossing points, footpaths, guardrails, waiting areas
- Roadside safety: Clear zone width, barrier requirements, hazard removal
- Signage and marking: Retroreflectivity, placement height, advance warning distances

## Design Consistency Checks
- Operating speed profile: Identify 85th percentile speed changes greater than 10 km/h
- Curve radius consistency: Avoid substandard curves after long tangents
- Sight distance verification: Stopping sight distance at every point on alignment
- Decision sight distance: At complex junctions, interchanges, and conflict points
- Cross-section transitions: Gradual lane width reductions, shoulder width changes

## Junction Safety Design
- Roundabouts: Deflection angle 45-75 degrees, entry width 3.5-4.5 m, circulatory width
- Signalized junctions: Intergreen time calculation, all-red clearance, detector placement
- Priority junctions: Major road speed reduction measures, ghost islands for right turns
- Grade-separated junctions: Merge and diverge lengths, acceleration/deceleration lanes
- Mini-roundabouts: For urban areas with speed limits below 50 km/h, raised domes

## Roadside Hazard Management
- Clear zone width: 3-10 m depending on design speed and traffic volume
- Hazard identification: Trees, poles, culvert headwalls, bridge piers, steep embankments
- Barrier selection: W-beam (flexible), concrete (rigid), cable (semi-rigid) for different needs
- Barrier placement: Deflection distance, end terminal treatment, approach length
- Crash cushions: For fixed hazards that cannot be removed or shielded by barrier

## Vulnerable Road User Consideration
- Pedestrian crossings: Marked crosswalks at junctions, mid-block where desire lines exist
- Footpath provision: Minimum 1.5 m width on both sides in urban areas
- Cycle facilities: Dedicated cycle lanes (1.5 m), shared paths, cycle parking
- Motorcycle safety: Separate lanes where volumes exceed 1000 motorcycles per day
- Public transport: Bus bays (3 m width, 30-50 m taper), shelter placement for safety

## Speed Management
- Speed humps: Height 75-100 mm, length 3-4 m, spacing 100-200 m in residential areas
- Raised crosswalks: Plateau type, 75 mm height, with pedestrian refuge islands
- Chicanes: Horizontal deflection, single lane width 3.0 m, stagger 5-10 m
- Gateway treatments: Entry features at settlement boundaries, speed reduction markings
- Variable speed limits: Electronic signs for school zones, weather conditions, work zones

## Safety Audit Reporting
- Problem description: Location, road user type, collision type, severity potential
- Evidence: Standards reference, accident data, design departures
- Recommendation: Specific mitigation measure with location and responsibility
- Severity rating: Catastrophic, critical, marginal, or negligible categorisation
- Client response: Accepted, accepted with modification, or rejected with justification
