# Construction Site Safety Management

## Legal Framework
- Health and Safety at Work Act: Employer duty to ensure safety of employees and public
- Construction (Design and Management) Regulations: Designer and contractor duties
- Factories Act: Workplace safety, machinery guarding, welfare facilities
- Workmen's Compensation Act: Employer liability for workplace injuries
- National Building Code: Structural safety during construction stage
- ILO Convention 167: International standards for construction safety

## Roles and Responsibilities
- Project manager: Overall responsibility for site safety, resource allocation
- Site safety officer: Dedicated safety professional, inspections, training, incident investigation
- Supervisor: Daily safety of work crews, toolbox talks, permit compliance
- Worker: Comply with safety rules, use PPE, report hazards
- Subcontractor: Safety plan, trained workforce, compliance with main contractor's policy
- Designer: Design out hazards, specify safe construction methods, risk assessment

## Hazard Identification
- Working at height: Scaffolding (designed and inspected), ladders (restricted to light access)
- Excavation collapse: Shoring, battering, trench boxes, daily inspection before entry
- Plant and machinery: Crushing, overturning, reversing, overhead services contact
- Lifting operations: Crane overturn, dropped loads, rigging failure
- Electricity: Overhead power lines, underground cables, portable electrical equipment
- Confined spaces: Manholes, tanks, tunnels, oxygen deficiency, toxic gases
- Manual handling: Lifting technique, weight limits, mechanical aids for loads over 25 kg

## Control Measures Hierarchy
- Elimination: Design to avoid hazardous activities (e.g., prefabricate at ground level)
- Substitution: Replace hazardous material/process with safer alternative
- Engineering controls: Guarding, ventilation, noise enclosures, fall arrest systems
- Administrative controls: Safe systems of work, training, signage, permits
- Personal protective equipment: Hard hat, safety boots, hi-vis vest, gloves, goggles

## Specific Safety Procedures
- Excavation safety: Batter slopes (1:1 in stable soil, 1.5:1 in sandy soil), spoil distance 1 m from edge
- Scaffolding: Designed by engineer, inspected weekly, tagged with safe load capacity
- Formwork: Designed for concrete pressure, minimum falsework factor of safety 2.0
- Demolition: Sequential removal, dust suppression, debris chutes, exclusion zones
- Piling: Noise and vibration monitoring, pile cage handling, piling rig stability
- Steel erection: Column guys until frame stable, bolted connections completed before releasing crane

## Fire Safety on Site
- Fire risk assessment: Identify ignition sources, combustible materials, escape routes
- Hot work permit: Welding, cutting, grinding permits with fire watch and extinguisher
- Flammable storage: Separate locked compound, ventilation, bonding/grounding for drums
- Fire extinguishers: Water (wood/paper), CO2 (electrical), foam (flammable liquids), powder (general)
- Assembly points: Designated safe area, signage, head count procedure
- Emergency plan: Fire warden, alarm testing (weekly), evacuation drill (quarterly)

## First Aid and Medical Facilities
- First aid box: Minimum one per 25 workers, contents checked monthly
- First aider: One per 50 workers on site, trained in construction first aid
- First aid room: For sites with 100+ workers, accessible to ambulance
- Accident reporting: RIDDOR reportable injuries (fatalities, major injuries, 7+ day absence)
- Occupational health: Hearing conservation, respiratory protection, dermatitis prevention
- Site clinic: For large projects, nurse on duty, paramedic for remote locations

## Safety Inspection and Audit
- Daily inspection: Excavations, scaffolding, electrical equipment by supervisor
- Weekly inspection: General site safety by project manager and safety officer
- Monthly audit: Formal review by senior management, documented findings
- External audit: Insurance company, regulatory authority, or client safety team
- Key records: Incident register, inspection reports, training records, permit files
- Performance indicators: Lost time injury frequency rate (LTIFR), near miss reporting rate

## Safety Training
- Induction training: Mandatory for all workers before starting on site, includes emergency procedures
- Toolbox talks: 10-15 minute daily sessions on specific hazards and safe procedures
- Competency certification: Scaffold erectors, crane operators, riggers, banksmen
- Refresher training: Annual for fire safety, first aid certificates renewed every 3 years
- Supervisor training: IOSH Managing Safely or equivalent, site safety supervisor certificate
