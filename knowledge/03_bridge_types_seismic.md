# Bridge Types and Seismic Design

## Bridge Classification by Span Length
- Culverts: Span less than 5 m, box or pipe configuration
- Slab bridges: Span 5-15 m, solid reinforced concrete slab
- T-beam bridges: Span 15-30 m, reinforced concrete girders with deck slab
- Box girder bridges: Span 25-60 m, single or multi-cell prestressed concrete
- Segmental bridges: Span 50-150 m, precast or cast-in-situ segments
- Cable-stayed bridges: Span 100-600 m, fan or harp cable arrangement
- Suspension bridges: Span 300-2000 m, main cables in catenary

## Bridge Deck Types
- Cast-in-situ reinforced concrete slab: Simple construction, short spans
- Precast prestressed I-girders: Rapid construction, standard sections
- Steel plate girders: Medium to long spans, composite with concrete deck
- Steel box girders: High torsional stiffness, long spans, curved alignments
- Orthotropic steel deck: Lightweight, long spans, integral with girders

## Superstructure Design
- Deck slab thickness: L/16 to L/20 for simply supported, L/22 to L/28 for continuous
- Girder spacing: 1.5-3.0 m for concrete, 2.5-5.0 m for steel girders
- Camber: Dead load deflection + 50% live load deflection for precast girders
- Continuity: 5-10% reduction in positive moment, 40-60% negative moment over piers
- Temperature effects: Uniform temperature range +-20 deg C for design

## Substructure Design
- Abutments: Gravity (mass concrete), cantilever (reinforced), spill-through (open)
- Piers: Solid wall, hammerhead, multi-column bent, pier types for different heights
- Pier caps: Distribute loads from superstructure bearings to columns
- Bearings: Elastomeric (simple), pot bearings (large rotations), spherical (seismic)
- Expansion joints: Finger joints (large movements), strip seals (small-medium movement)

## Seismic Design Principles
- Seismic zones: Zone 1 (0.1g) to Zone 4 (0.4g) based on peak ground acceleration
- Response spectrum: Acceleration vs period curve for elastic design
- Ductility: Capacity design approach, plastic hinges form in columns not beams
- Isolation: Lead rubber bearings to increase period and reduce seismic forces
- Damping: 5% viscous damping typical for reinforced concrete bridges

## Seismic Detailing Requirements
- Column confinement: Spiral or hoop reinforcement at 100 mm max spacing in plastic hinge zone
- Plastic hinge length: 0.08L + 0.15fy db for columns, minimum 1.5D
- Lap splices: Avoid in plastic hinge zones, provide full tension splices
- Shear reinforcement: Capacity design, shear demand based on plastic moment capacity
- Seat width: Minimum 400 mm at abutments to prevent unseating
- Restrainers: Cable restrainers at expansion joints to limit relative displacement

## Foundation Types
- Spread footings: For good soil conditions, shallow depth, bearing capacity > 200 kPa
- Pile foundations: For poor soils, high lateral loads, scour susceptible sites
- Driven piles: Concrete (250-500 mm square), steel H-piles, timber piles
- Bored cast-in-situ piles: Diameter 600-2000 mm, socketed into rock for scour resistance
- Well foundations: Caissons, 5-15 m diameter, for major river bridges

## Scour Design
- Scour depth equation: ds = 2.2 K1 K2 y1^0.35 V1^0.43 / g^0.35 for pier scour
- Contraction scour: Increased velocity due to bridge constriction of waterway
- Abutment scour: Local scour at abutment ends, depends on embankment configuration
- Scour countermeasures: Riprap (D50 = 0.5-1.0 m), gabion mattresses, concrete aprons
- Minimum foundation depth: Below maximum scour depth + 2.0 m for safety
- Scour monitoring: Bed level sensors, annual bathymetric surveys after major floods

## Inspection and Load Rating
- Routine inspection: Annual visual inspection of all bridge elements
- Principal inspection: Detailed inspection every 5 years, hands-on for critical areas
- Load rating: Operating rating and inventory rating per AASHTO or BS 5400
- Rating factor: RF = (Capacity - Dead Load) / Live Load, RF > 1.0 for safe operation
- Fatigue evaluation: For steel bridges, remaining fatigue life calculation
