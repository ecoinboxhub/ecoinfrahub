# Construction Materials Testing Laboratory

## Aggregate Testing
- Sieve analysis: Particle size distribution, grading curves, fineness modulus
- Specific gravity: Pycnometer method for fine aggregates, basket method for coarse
- Water absorption: Percentage of water absorbed after 24-hour soaking
- Aggregate crushing value (ACV): Maximum 30% for base course, 25% for surface course
- Los Angeles abrasion value: Maximum 40% for base, 35% for surface, 50% for subbase
- Flakiness and elongation index: Maximum 35% combined for pavement aggregates
- Soundness test: Sodium sulfate or magnesium sulfate for freeze-thaw resistance
- Organic impurities: Color test for sand, darker than standard indicates contamination

## Concrete Testing
- Slump test: 25-50 mm for pavements, 50-100 mm for general reinforced concrete, 100-150 mm for congested sections
- Compressive strength: 150 mm cubes tested at 7 days (65% of 28-day) and 28 days
- Flexural strength: Beam test (500x100x100 mm), modulus of rupture for pavement design
- Tensile splitting: Brazilian test on 150 mm diameter cylinders for crack control design
- Air content: Pressure method for freeze-thaw resistance of air-entrained concrete
- Unit weight: Fresh and hardened density for dead load and quality control
- Water-cement ratio: w/c ratio directly affects strength and durability
- Chloride penetration: Rapid chloride permeability test (RCPT) for durability assessment

## Cement Testing
- Standard consistency: Vicat apparatus, normal consistency (25-33% water)
- Setting time: Initial set minimum 45 minutes, final set maximum 10 hours (EN 197-1)
- Soundness: Le Chatelier apparatus, expansion less than 10 mm
- Compressive strength: Mortar cubes (40x40x160 mm), tested at 2, 7, and 28 days
- Fineness: Specific surface area by Blaine air permeability method (minimum 225 m2/kg for OPC)
- Heat of hydration: Isothermal calorimetry or semi-adiabatic method for mass concrete

## Steel Reinforcement Testing
- Tensile strength: Yield stress (fy), ultimate tensile strength, elongation percentage
- Bend test: 180-degree bending around mandrel diameter specified by standard
- Re-bend test: Bend, age, then reverse bend to check aging brittleness
- Weldability: Carbon equivalent (CEV) calculation, CEV less than 0.41 for weldable grade
- Rib geometry: Rib height, spacing, inclination for bond strength
- Mass per meter: Tolerance +-4.5% for bar diameter verification
- Fatigue test: For seismic-grade reinforcement, 2 million cycles at specified stress range

## Soil Testing
- Natural moisture content: Oven drying at 105-110 deg C for 24 hours
- Atterberg limits: Liquid limit (Casagrande cup), plastic limit (thread rolling), plasticity index
- Particle size distribution: Sieve analysis for coarse fraction, hydrometer for fines below 75 micron
- Proctor compaction: Standard (600 kNm/m3) and Modified (2700 kNm/m3) compaction energy
- CBR test: Soaked (4 days) and unsoaked, for pavement design, 5 mm penetration value
- Specific gravity: Density bottle method for fine-grained soils
- Direct shear: Quick (undrained) and consolidated drained tests for shear strength parameters
- Consolidation: Oedometer test for compression index, preconsolidation pressure, coefficient of consolidation

## Bitumen and Asphalt Testing
- Penetration: Needle penetration at 25 deg C, grade determined (e.g., 60/70, 80/100)
- Softening point: Ring and ball test, higher softening point for hot climates
- Ductility: Distance elongated before breaking at 25 deg C, minimum 100 cm
- Viscosity: Rotational viscometer for mixing and compaction temperatures
- Flash and fire point: Cleveland open cup, safety during heating operations
- Marshall stability: Stability (minimum 8 kN), flow (2-4 mm), stability/flow ratio
- Bitumen content: Centrifuge extraction for mix design verification
- Air voids: Percentage of voids in total mix (VTM) for durability, 3-5% target
- Indirect tensile strength: Moisture susceptibility, tensile strength ratio minimum 80%

## Quality Control Procedures
- Sampling frequency: One sample per 50 m3 of concrete, per 100 tonnes of aggregate
- Hold points: Concrete pour, steel fixing, backfilling require inspection before proceeding
- Non-conformance reporting: Identify, document, assess, and rectify all defects
- Calibration schedule: All testing equipment calibrated annually or per manufacturer
- Test record retention: Minimum 5 years for all quality control test results
- Standards compliance: BS EN, ASTM, ISO, or national standards as specified in contract
