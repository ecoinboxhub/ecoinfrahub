# Nigerian Highway Manual - Key Design Standards

## Design Speed
- Rural highways: 80-100 km/h
- Urban highways: 60-80 km/h
- Local roads: 30-50 km/h

## Pavement Design
- Design life: 20 years for flexible pavements
- CBR design: Using 90th percentile of soaked CBR values
- Minimum base thickness: 150mm for light traffic, 200mm for medium, 250mm for heavy
- Asphalt concrete surface: 40-50mm wearing course, 50-100mm binder course

## Traffic Classification
- Light: < 0.5 million ESA
- Medium: 0.5 - 5 million ESA
- Heavy: 5 - 30 million ESA
- Very Heavy: > 30 million ESA

## Drainage Requirements
- Minimum longitudinal gradient: 0.5%
- Maximum longitudinal gradient: 8% for rural highways
- Side drain minimum depth: 450mm
- Culvert minimum diameter: 900mm for main roads

## Geometric Standards
- Lane width: 3.5m for rural highways, 3.0m for urban
- Shoulder width: 2.5m paved, 3.0m unpaved
- Minimum horizontal curve radius: Based on design speed
- Superelevation: Maximum 8%
- Stopping sight distance: Based on design speed per AASHTO

## Materials
- Subgrade CBR: Minimum 5% for highway construction
- Selected fill: CBR > 10%
- Subbase: CBR > 30%
- Base course: CBR > 80% or 100% CBR for heavy traffic
- Asphalt penetration grade: 60/70 for hot climate regions

# Climate Adaptation for Road Infrastructure

## Flood Resilience
- Raise embankment height 0.5m above 100-year flood level
- Provide adequate culvert capacity for 50-year storm events
- Use permeable pavements in low-lying areas
- Install scour protection at bridge abutments

## Heat Resilience
- Use lighter-colored pavement surfaces to reduce heat absorption
- Select asphalt binder with higher softening point for hot regions
- Provide shade trees along urban road corridors
- Use reflective coatings on bridge decks

## Drainage Design for Climate Change
- Increase design storm frequency from 1-in-10 to 1-in-50 years
- Add 20% capacity margin for future rainfall intensity increase
- Design detention basins for 1-in-100 year storms
- Use grassed waterways for erosion control

## Sustainable Materials
- Reclaimed Asphalt Pavement (RAP): Up to 30% in new mixes
- Recycled concrete aggregate for base and subbase layers
- Geopolymer concrete for reduced carbon footprint
- Plastic-modified asphalt for improved durability
