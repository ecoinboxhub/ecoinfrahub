# Water Supply Distribution Systems

## Distribution Network Types
- Branching (dead-end) system: Simple layout, low cost, dead ends cause stagnation
- Gridiron system: Interconnected pipes, no dead ends, better pressure equalization
- Ring system: Closed loops, continuous circulation, best for fire fighting
- Combined system: Ring main with branch feeders, most common in urban areas

## Design Parameters
- Peak factor: 2.0-2.5 for residential, 1.5-2.0 for industrial areas
- Minimum service pressure: 15-20 m head for single story, 25-30 m for multi-story
- Maximum velocity: 1.5-2.0 m/s to prevent water hammer and scour
- Design period: 20-30 years for pipes, 15-20 years for pumps
- Per capita demand: 60-120 L/person/day for rural areas, 120-200 for urban

## Pipe Materials
- uPVC: Lightweight, corrosion resistant, low cost, suitable for buried mains
- Ductile iron: High strength, durable, suitable for high pressure and road crossings
- HDPE: Flexible, jointless by fusion welding, good for rough terrain
- Steel: Highest strength, used for exposed crossings and pump mains
- Asbestos cement: Banned in many countries due to health concerns

## Storage Requirements
- Elevated storage: Provides gravity pressure, minimum 24-hour capacity
- Ground level storage: For treatment plant clear well and pumping stations
- Fire storage: Based on fire flow (15-30 L/s) for 2-4 hour duration
- Emergency storage: 25-50% of average daily demand for breakdown periods

## Hydraulic Analysis
- Hazen-Williams formula: v = 0.85 C R^0.63 S^0.54
- C values: uPVC/HDPE = 140-150, ductile iron = 130-140, steel = 100-120
- Pressure class ratings: PN 6, PN 10, PN 16 (bar) for different service conditions
- Water hammer pressure: Delta P = rho c Delta v, c = wave speed in pipe material
- Minimum cover depth: 1.0 m to top of pipe for frost protection and traffic loading

## Appurtenances
- Gate valves: For section isolation, installed at 500-1000 m intervals
- Air release valves: At high points to release trapped air
- Washout/scour valves: At low points for sediment flushing
- Fire hydrants: At 150-200 m spacing in urban areas
- Pressure reducing valves: Where ground elevation drops exceed 50 m
- Flow meters: Electromagnetic or ultrasonic for bulk metering

## Service Connections
- Typical connection: Corporation stop at main, service pipe, meter, ferrule
- Meter types: Positive displacement for residential, compound for commercial
- Minimum service pipe size: 15 mm (1/2 inch) for residential
- Backflow prevention: Required on all connections with cross-contamination risk

## Rural and Community Systems
- Standpipe/public tap design: One tap per 200-500 people maximum
- Hand pump boreholes: India Mark II/III, Afridev, Vergnet types
- Solar pumping: 2-10 kW panels for boreholes in off-grid areas
- Reticulation: HDPE pipes laid in trenches, 0.75-1.0 m minimum cover
- Community management: Water point committees, tariff collection, maintenance fund
