# User Personas — EcoInfraMind AI

## Persona 1: Kofi Mensah — Rural Highway Engineer

**Demographics**
- Age: 38
- Role: Senior Highway Engineer, Ghana Highway Authority
- Location: Kumasi, Ghana (field assignments across rural Ashanti Region)
- Education: BSc Civil Engineering, MSc Transportation Engineering
- Technical proficiency: Moderate — comfortable with engineering software but not a developer

**Work Environment**
- Spends 60% of time in field offices with unstable internet
- Carries a company-issued Windows laptop (Core i5, 8 GB RAM)
- Needs quick access to design standards (Ghana Highway Manual, Eurocodes)
- Often works on emergency road repairs during rainy season

**Pain Points**
- Cannot access cloud-based engineering tools in remote areas
- Expensive and slow to consult senior engineers in the capital
- Lacks a quick way to compute pavement thickness, drainage, or earthwork volumes in the field
- Paper-based standards are bulky and outdated

**Goals**
- Get immediate answers to technical questions without internet
- Run engineering calculations on-site
- Access contextual advice referencing local standards
- Generate maintenance reports quickly

**How EcoInfraMind AI Helps**
- Offline chat answers engineering questions using RAG from road-design knowledge base
- Built-in calculators for pavement, drainage, and earthwork
- Streaming responses allow partial answers even before full generation completes
- Expert assistant mode provides highway-specific guidance
